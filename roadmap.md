## 2. Topic Clusters & Hierarchy

1. **Cluster A: Fundamental Arithmetic & Conditionals (01–17)**
   - Basic I/O, operators, math library, leap year, temperature conversion, character case, vowel checks.
2. **Cluster B: Loop Iterations & Number Theory (18–30)**
   - Prime, perfect, strong, Armstrong, palindrome, digit sum, reverse, factorial, Fibonacci, GCD/LCM.
3. **Cluster C: Series Calculations & Mathematics (31–44)**
   - Factorial sums, arithmetic progressions, geometric progressions, harmonic fractions, power series.
4. **Cluster D: Pattern Printing Masterclass (Patterns 1–59)**
   - Ascending left triangles, descending triangles, right-aligned triangles, diamond shapes, rectangular grids, counting progressions, and centered pyramids.
5. **Cluster E: Recursion, Arrays & Jump Statements (53–60)**
   - Recursive algorithms, array maximum/minimum, pointer manipulation concepts, continue/break controls.
